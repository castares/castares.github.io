This is a digital garden, not a blog. Notes here are published early and revised over time, so what you read may be half-formed. That's on purpose.

Each note shows how developed it is:

- 🌱 **Seedling**: a raw or early thought.
- 🌿 **Budding**: revisited and taking shape.
- 🌳 **Evergreen**: settled enough that I'd point people to it.

Notes link to each other. A dimmed, dashed link like [[Why gardens beat blogs]] points to a thought I haven't written yet.
