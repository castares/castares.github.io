# Personal Website

César Castañón's personal site: an introduction, where to find César online, and a public space for unfinished thinking.

## Language

### Site

**Home**:
The single landing page: headline, short bio, Profile Links, and the most recently Tended Notes.
_Avoid_: Landing, about page, index

**Profile Links**:
The handful of external profiles shown on Home (currently GitHub, LinkedIn, Bluesky).
_Avoid_: Socials, contact

### Garden

**Garden**:
The public collection of Notes, organised by connection and topic rather than by date.
_Avoid_: Blog, journal, articles section

**Note**:
A single piece of writing in the Garden that may be unfinished and may be revised at any time after publishing.
_Avoid_: Post, article, blog post, entry

**Maturity**:
How developed a Note is: one of Seedling, Budding, or Evergreen. A Note without a stated Maturity is a Seedling.
_Avoid_: Status, stage, progress

**Seedling**:
A Note that is a raw or early thought.

**Budding**:
A Note that has been revisited and is taking shape.

**Evergreen**:
A Note the author considers settled enough to point people to.

**Planted**:
A Note is Planted when it is first published to the Garden.
_Avoid_: Posted, created, published-on

**Tended**:
A Note is Tended when it is meaningfully revised after being Planted; "recently tended" orders Notes by their last such revision. Trivial fixes do not count.
_Avoid_: Updated, edited, modified

**Unplanted Link**:
A link from a Note to a Note that does not exist yet; it marks a thought not yet written, not an error.
_Avoid_: Broken link, dead link, red link

**Draft**:
A Note in the Garden that is explicitly held back from publishing.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, private note
